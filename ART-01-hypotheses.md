# ART-01 — Hypothesis Formulation (pack baseline)

**Protocol pin:** `HVHR-IBE-RB-1.1`  
**Role:** Three locked identities for new runs — **H-R**, **H-A**, **H-V**. No scores. Stage 1 locks quoted atomics; file is READ-ONLY for the remainder of a run unless owner version-bumps.

## Locked H-R (quoted atomic)

> God raised Jesus from death into transformed, glorified bodily life, with genuine personal and bodily continuity with the Jesus who died. The risen Jesus himself caused postmortem encounters. These encounters, their interpretation within their historical setting—including available Jewish categories—and the transmission of testimony generated and stabilized resurrection belief and proclamation, appearance traditions, the early creed and appearance list, and the continuing movement. Ordinary transmission, elaboration, and literary shaping may account for later forms where supported by the locked evidence.

## Locked H-A — Appearance class (quoted atomic)

> Jesus was not raised into transformed bodily life. Reported postmortem encounters identified as Jesus arose through one or more **pre-registered** appearance-type pathways (not an open “or otherwise”). Admissible subpaths are frozen in advance before scoring; examples include ordinary perceptual / misperception pathways; internally generated hallucinatory / visionary (non-veridical) pathways; veridical extra-mental apparition (**H-V**); and mixed appearance models composing only pre-registered subpaths. H-A may not switch subpaths ad hoc per datum without paying C4 / C6 / C7 costs. Fraud / deception is **not** the whole of H-A unless separately registered as its own pathway. These encounters, their interpretation within their historical setting—including available Jewish categories—and the transmission of testimony generated and stabilized resurrection belief and proclamation, appearance traditions, the early creed and appearance list, and the continuing movement. Ordinary transmission, elaboration, and literary shaping may account for later forms where supported by the locked evidence.

## Locked H-V (quoted atomic)

> After Jesus’s death, persons underwent phenomenally actual apparitions identified as Jesus, in a visionary mode. These apparitions had substantive veridical content grounded in a real extra-mental correlate, whose nature remains unspecified here; mere experiential occurrence or sincerity does not fulfill that commitment. They occurred without Jesus having been raised into transformed bodily life and without requiring a surviving empirical personality of Jesus as their causal agent. These encounters, their interpretation within their historical setting—including available Jewish categories—and the transmission of testimony generated and stabilized resurrection belief and proclamation, appearance traditions, the early creed and appearance list, and the continuing movement. Ordinary transmission, elaboration, and literary shaping may account for later forms where supported by the locked evidence.

**Relation:** **H-V is the restricted veridical subpath of H-A** — the harder anti-H-R rival when extra-mental veridical appearance is granted. H-V remains a separately scored identity (Court 2); it is not a free rewrite of H-A mid-run.

## Two courts (required)

Stage 7 scores **two pairwise courts**, not a single three-way blended winner:

1. **Court 1: H-R vs H-A** — Do we need bodily resurrection at all? (appearance-class rival)
2. **Court 2: H-R vs H-V** — Even granting extra-mental veridical appearance, is raised body better? (restricted veridical rival)

Ordinal labels are court-specific (see ART-02). Anti-abstention and the three weight lenses apply **per court**.

## Mode ≠ source

Encounter **mode** is not causal **source**. H-R may include visionary encounters *caused by* the bodily risen Jesus. Visionary phenomenology alone does not decide for H-A or H-V. Mode ≠ source is retained for all three identities.
