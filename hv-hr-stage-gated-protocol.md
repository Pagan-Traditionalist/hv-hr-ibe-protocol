### 2.3 Owner Neutrality Lock (10) — 1.1 clarified

**Neutrality means:**

1. **(a) Do not cook C1–C8** (or any locked criteria) to privilege H-R or H-V.  
2. **(b) Do not cherry-pick or pad the record** for either side.

**Neutrality does *not* mean:** abstaining from comparative labels (`H-R+` / `H-V+` / `≈`) when an ordinal difference is clear after comparing outstanding burdens. Withholding labels out of “fairness theater” is **abstention**, and is a protocol defect under 1.1.

Continuing locks:

1. **Symmetric standards** — same evidence packet, same criteria lock, same artifact templates for H-V and H-R. Shared mechanisms cancel.  
2. **Steelmans, not cartoons** — H-R is full academic transformed bodily resurrection (not resuscitation caricature). H-V is complete and vulnerable (not empty-correlate escape). Dual steelman H-R vs H-V is the **default**.  
3. **Criteria before builds** — freeze IBE rulers before either memorandum.  
4. **No hidden destination** — failure, underdetermination, H-R ahead (esp. under C), and H-V ahead are all legitimate. Destination is a ceiling to test, not a premise to preserve.  
5. **No hidden naturalism / no hidden lean-TI at B** — B stays metaphysical agnosticism + shared MF. Do not smuggle T/G/C priors into B.  
6. **C is a real steelman seat** — authorization + expected vindication prior must be usable; protocol must not quietly make H-R unwinnable. Bodily MODE is **not** locked by C.  
7. **Hostile evidence tested** — empty tomb / strong group claims are Contested or sensitivity, never permanently erased; grant them for named sensitivity passes.  
8. **Research seat does not crown a winner** — comparative auditor ≠ either constructor.  
9. **Publishable prompts** — launch prompts and locks printable as-is; no secret side instructions.  
10. **Fairness self-check** — explicit hostile-Christian-reader checklist with Pass criteria (Neutrality Gate, §12), including anti-abstention.

### 2.4 Hard success conditions (3) — constitutive

The protocol fails design review unless it guarantees all three:

1. **Dual steelman** — H-V and H-R each get full academic strength (parity, specificity, self-risk, matched downstream social mechanisms). Neither cartooned, emptied, or softened.  
2. **Fairest abductive criteria** — researched and frozen *before* builds; literature-derived; symmetric; ordinal; no post-hoc rulers; shared mechanisms cancel; discriminating rows explicit; weighting challenges recorded; hostile-reader Pass on the criteria themselves. Criteria must not be cooked to privilege either hyp.  
3. **No relevant-data omission** — total-evidence discipline: Christian historical record *and* relevant background (apparition/bereavement comparanda, Jewish categories, miracle epistemology, strongest H-R *and* H-V scholarship). Contested → Contested/sensitivity, never silent drop. Excluded needs an explicit reason. Gaps named. Tomb/group and other H-R-favorable claims must appear somewhere **testable**. Do not cherry-pick or pad for either side.

The thin Neutrality Gate (§12) **must check these three explicitly**, plus anti-abstention.

### 2.5 Symmetric steelman (default)

- **H-R:** transformed bodily resurrection (not resuscitation cartoon); divine action; continuity; tomb/group/Paul/James as the evidence warrants; same downstream social processes; auxiliaries and losing conditions explicit.  
- **H-V:** complete veridical-apparition social-formation (not empty-correlate escape; not fraud cartoon; not “mass hallucination” label); bounded group pathway; interpretive bridge; positive support for extra-mental correlate stated and **costed**; auxiliaries and losing conditions explicit; no named metaphysics as scoring premise.

Neither side is weakened to help the other. Neutrality ≠ stacking for H-V; steelman H-V ≠ stacking against Christianity. Dual steelman is the default posture of Stage 6.
